aboutbio · contact · poem

Cinematographer + Artist + Spatial Designer

he/him · Vancouver

A Crəə-Métis multidisciplinary artist, Gill James works across cinematography, photography, spatial and object design, sound and live performance, and sees them as one practice. His artful eye and practical nature fuse art, natural materials and function; increasingly his work leaves the hand and enters the land.

As I mature as a Crəə-Métis artist, my many meanderings through various media have led to a diverse portfolio, blending fine art, dance, music, filmmaking and industrial design. Now, I look forward to combining all these diverse elements into meaningful opportunities to connect culturally, to tell stories that reflect my heritage, and to uplift Indigenous voices through visual storytelling.

All my relations.
