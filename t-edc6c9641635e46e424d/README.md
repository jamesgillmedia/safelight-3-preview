# oz · 80s mode (whole-app preview)

**Reduce Motion.** oz ignores the iPhone Reduce Motion flag. Scanlines, icon life, reels, the clock, teletype, and glow keep running when the flag is on.

Hidden entry: hold the mint dot ~520ms for the colour / lightness pad; slide past dim, or hold the centre notch ~420ms, to reveal the 80s rocker. Off by default. When on, the flavour is **Terminal** unless you pick Neon or 1-bit on the sub-switch beside the rocker. A short ready line — oz READY — plays once and settles. BRIGHT and CONTRAST knobs on the right actually dim and contrast the screen.

Icons live on Home and in the dock through the labelled oz viewer hook (a preview stub — not the real viewer engine).

Open `index.html`. Frames: `?shot=home|dock|dial|phone|sound|weather|clock|icons&flav=terminal|neon|bit`.
